Straight talk. The churn model scores about 96% accuracy (a neural network gets 97.0% on a 630-customer test, and the Arabic comment reader is right about 83% of the time when it commits to a side) on customers it has never seen, tested on the public Iranian telecom dataset (3,150 customers). Your own data may behave differently, so treat the scores as a way to prioritize calls, not as certainty.
